Based on 

> Value = <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">Desired Outcome x Likely hood</mark> / <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Time Delay x Effort</mark>

You want to increase the green and lower the red. When you craft an offer you should think of these variables. 

The key to making good offers is that the customers need to **perceive** these values. [[Marketing is about increasing perceived values]]
