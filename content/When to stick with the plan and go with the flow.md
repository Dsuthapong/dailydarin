---
title: When to stick with the plan and go with the flow
---
#permanent 

- Stick with the plan: 
	- Commitment to someone else
	- Deadline that you're not willing to let you of the result (revenue impact? personal impact?)
	- You know you should do now but it feels uncomfortable
- Go with the flow 
	- Things that drawn you is important and you're in rare state of high motivation
