title: Cluster of Deception
---
#permanent/concept 
>"A Multiple indication of deception that happen at the same time and are different from the person's baseline"
> 

For example, if we see the below behaviors excessively, and these are different from the person's baseline, it might indicate that they are lying
- [[Lip Biting can indicate deception]]
- [[Slow Blink could indicate a deception]]
- [[Eye flutter is an indication of deception]]
- [[Movement incongruity could indicate a deception]]
- [[Duping Delight]]
