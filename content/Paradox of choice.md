---
title: Paradox of choice
---
It is the concept that we don't make decisions if they are too many options. This is backed up by a study that, one group of people were presented with 4 options of Jams. The other group was presented with many options. The first group made more decisions. 

[See Details](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Paradox_of_Choice)
