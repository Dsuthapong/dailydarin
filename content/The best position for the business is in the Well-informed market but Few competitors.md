title: The best position for the business is in the Well-informed market but Few competitors
---
#permanent 
Many businesses want to innovate, but the hard part of being in the Blue Ocean is communicating the new value you bring to the market. 
This is why the "second or third" options in the market, like Google or iPhone are the ones dominating the market because: 
1. The market is already educated with the solution (Google - Search engine, iPhone - SmartPhone like Black Berry)
2. There were few players in the market. And those are not so good.
