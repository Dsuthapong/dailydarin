---
title: Use Inversion technique to make better decisions
---
Inversion is a technique used by stoics ([[Stoicsm]]) to make decisions. 

To use this technique, consider "What are possible reasons why you won't achieve a goal" in order to make better plans. 
For example, instead of asking "What I need to go to achieve this goal," you ask "What I need to do to NOT achieve this goal"
