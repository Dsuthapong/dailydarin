#permanent/concept 
From the . He mentioned that most people believe that if they are 1% better each day, it compounds and in 365 days, they have 10X better!
For example, if they read and learn everyday for 10 mins, in a year they will be much smarter!

However, Justin challenges that you have to ==measure== the result in order for you to know that you're 1% better! You can't just assume that reading 10 mins each day means you're 1% better. It's back to the core principle of productivity which is [[To be productive, you need to optimize for the "Outcome" or the "Output"]]
