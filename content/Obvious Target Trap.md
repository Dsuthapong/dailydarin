---
title: Obvious Target Trap
---
#permanent/concept 
It's the concept from  that sometimes we optimize for something that is obvious to us but doesn't really help us move to the goal. 

For example, if the Notion template fails to do X, the obvious thing to do is to fix that template to be able to do X. However, doing that might not be the most "productive" thing as that might be other things we can do that help us reach the "product" or the "outcome" that we want.
