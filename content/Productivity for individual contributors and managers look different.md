---
title: Productivity for individual contributors and managers look different
---
#permanent 
While an individual contributors is about having time for himself and getting his own work done, a manager gets work done through other people. So his productivity doesn't come from "his work" but the ability to help the team becomes productive. 

For example, for a manager, most of the time you need to [[Check communication first to start a productive day| unblock your team first thing in the morning,]] while an individual contributor may [[Do hard things first in the morning to kick of a productive day|start the day by focusing on her task first]]
