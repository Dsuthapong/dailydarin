> [!NOTE]- **Facts about work** 
> - Work will fill up a large part of your life
> - Work, by its nature, is for "others" not for you
> -  This means that how you get rewarded is solely based on how much value you can deliver
> - The more value you can give (better performance), the more reward
> - Work (for most people) has core fucntions of paying billing, and support your living 

## Summary
Different people define passion differently, and there is no simple answer to this. Here's what I've learnt so far: 
### 1. You should be good at what you do .. No matter what 
- **No matter what, you should pursue a career that you can perform well and have natural inclination to doing that job.** For example, if you are a people person and have a really bad logic from a very young age, then coding might not be right for your. 
- This is because the nature of work is to "serve people and address their needs." So if you can't do a good job, maybe you should pursue other career that you have natural inclination. 

### 2. Try finding work that matches your life's needs 
Work is a big part of life. Life's key purpose is to be "satisfied" 
**So the key question is ==What do you want from work?==**
- Money
- Enjoyment
- Purpose
- Skills Development 
- Free time 

This will change overtime.
