#permanent 
Price is relative. People need to anchor price to something. What the anchor would be depends on: 
1.  - Driven by
	1. How often they need to buy this kinda thing
	2. Awareness of value you bring
	3. Information about competitors
	4. Priority: How urgent they need your product 
2. Competitive Landscape: How competitive is your market
