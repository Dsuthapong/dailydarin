title: "Practice \"Losing your hat\" before retirement"
---
#permanent 
As mentioned in :"ถอดหมวกมันเจ็บ."
After working, especially in a corporate environment, for a long time, when you "lose your hat," it hurts. 
You might used to have an assistant or a driver, and now you are an uncle doing everything yourself. 

Therefore, you need to practice living life simply, with no assistant, before you retire.
