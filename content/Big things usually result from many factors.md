---
title: Big things usually result from many factors
---
#permanent 

When people talk about [[Success]] and [[Failure]], they think there is "one" magical thing that causes a project, a business, or a relationship to fail. In reality, it rarely works that way. Things fall apart by the thousand cranks. The death comes from a thousand cuts. The tree grows into a beautiful and blooming one because of the effort that we put in over time.
