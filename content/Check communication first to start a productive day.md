title: Check communication first to start a productive day
---
#permanent 
Putting things in motion means unblocking other people so that they can go do stuff for you - related to [[Mie En Place]]. This is counter to the popular belief that we should "Eat the frog" first thing in the morning. By putting things in motion, other people can work for you in parallel while you're working on other things.

This is contrasted with [[Do hard things first in the morning to kick of a productive day]]
