---
title: Encouragement through gratitude, instead of Praise or Rebuke is the key to healthy interpersonal relationship
---
#permanent 
When you praise or rebuke, you are telling the other person that she has passed or not passed your judgement. This in a way sending signal to the other person that you are "above them."
Example: Saying "Good Boy" or "Great job" or "You are horrible" are all kinds of judmental statements 

Instead, since [[Each person is responsible for his own life]], the way have a good relationship is to instead "Encourage." This literally means "Bringing back the courage" by letting them know that they are worthy. To encourage, one must express gratitude, such as saying 
- "Thank you" or
- Pointing out how their contribution has helped you. [[When praising, make it specific to avoid coming across as insincere]]
