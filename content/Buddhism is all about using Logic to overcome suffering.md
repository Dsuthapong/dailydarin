#permanent 
The goal of Buddhism is to free people from suffering.
Unlike other religions, Buddhism use logics to help the mind understand things as they really are and free the mind from suffering. 

### Example
- When we're upset of our employees not performing as we planned, we need to understand the root cause the he only has limited capability and we will be less upset.
- When the mom says things that hurt us, we need to see why she says that and try to understand where she is coming from.
