#permanent 
Greed is the desire to get more results than what is reasonable. What is reasonable is the actions that can be done without any damage to anyone, yourself and others. When greed comes to work in your life. You don't feel good. Other people don't feel good. [[Diligent in Cause, Enjoy the Process, Let go of the results|You focus on the results than the cause]]
