---
title: Negative Thinking leads to negative feeling and Suffering
---
#permanent 
In Dharma, the mental formation is the key factor to suffering. The elements (รูป), Feelings (เวทนา), Perception (สัญญา), are only the fundamental ingredients, but the mental formation is the key ingredients that create Happiness and Sadness.
