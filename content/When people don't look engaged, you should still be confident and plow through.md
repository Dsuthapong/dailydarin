---
title: "When people don't look engaged, you should still be confident and plow through"
---
#permanent 
Sometime, when you deliver speech, or something and people seem to be bored, don't lose faith. Don't feel deflated. That attitude will only make things worst. 
Instead, you should Cheer Up! Only strong leaders can get people through and be excited regardless of the situation. And even if in the end they aren't feel motivated, it's okay. You just need to do your job anyway. You are not here to sell ice cream and please everyone.
