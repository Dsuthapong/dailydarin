---
title: Good writing is Clear
---
Being clear means that your message doesn't have many interpretations and the readers can "see" what you say.

---
- Provide clear opening and messaging 
- Provide Specific examples: Facts, Stats
- You should show, don't tell. 
	- **Wrong** - It's a beautiful day
	- **Right** - It's a sunny day with a light wind breeze in the air.
