---
title: Effective leader is emotionally stable
---
#permanent 
As a leader, you need to be exposed to a lot of emotional turbulence. You need to be able to withstand all the things that will be thrown at you, which could be a lot of not so pleasant things. Therefore, maintaining the emotional stability is very crucial to make sure that you can steer the ship in the right direction.
