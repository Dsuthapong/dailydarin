title: A good GPT Prompt consists of Task, Persona, Exemplar, Format, and Tone
---
#permanent 




 - Task: What to do 
 - Context: More information that is helpful
 - Exemplar: Great example
 - Persona: Who is chat (role)
 - Format: The deliverable format
 - Tone: The way that Chat GPT delivers information, i.e. in eight-grade language
