---
title: When choosing the career, consider the tasks instead of the label
---
#permanent 
When choosing a satisfying career, you should think about the day-to-day tasks that you can be "engaged" in, instead of the "title."
For example, instead of thinking I want to be a "doctor," think if you would enjoy the day-to-day tasks of being a doctor, which includes talking to strangers all the time, perform high-stress and precision-oriented tasks
