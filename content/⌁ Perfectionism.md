In a nutshell, perfectionist comes from a believe that your ==[[Self-Worth]] is strongly tied to the outcome of your performance==. This creates intense anxiety and lead to over controlling. [[⌁ Control]]. While it is great to do great work and strive for excellence in what you do, there is a big difference between "Perfectionism" and "Excellence"

- [[Excellence is rooted in mastery, whereas Perfectionism is rooted in anxiety]]
