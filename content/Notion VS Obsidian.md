#permanent 

- Notion is better at: 
	- *Presentation:* You can adjust layout however you want. Adding media is easy and beautiful. You can have tags and colors
	- *Managing information:* You can just select and drag information however you want, versus Obsidian where you have to use keyboard for moving stuff around
- Obsidian
	- It's fluid .. like a water stream.. it's fast.. 
	- It's quiet.. it's not connected to anything which makes it a powerful tool ..
