---
title: Manipulators are goal-oriented
---
#permanent 
They are fixate on the outcome no matter what. To get out of this ask 
=="What options do we have"==

For example,  if a guy is insisting on going home, you can ask "what options do we have" to see if he is manipulating, or at least get him out of the manipulation mode.

When we ask the questions, we flip the script so that we are on the offense, and they are on the defense. If they seem angry and really focus on the goals, you can be sure they are trying to manipulate you.
