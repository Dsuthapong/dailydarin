---
title: Asyncrhous communication
---
**Concept**
This is a concept that the person that send and receive information don't have to be available at the same time. The way to accomplish this is through Chat, Video Recording, and Audio Recording. 

The key premise is that "you send information when you're at your best, and the other person receives information when they're most ready"

---
**Practicality**
The key problem is that both parties need to have "discipline," especially the one receiving the message. Otherwise the other person might not find time to listen to the message.
