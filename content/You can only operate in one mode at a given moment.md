---
title: You can only operate in one mode at a given moment
---
In order to be productive, you need to select the mode to operate. For example, you can either operate in a follower mode or a leader mode. If you keep switching between follower and a leader, it's gonna be very stressful and not effective

### Each mode dictates following things: 
1. **Objectives:** What are you trying to achieve
2. **Focus:** Where you will spend energy most  
3. **Activities:** What you actually do 
4. **Environments and tools:** Place and stuff you need
