---
title: Leaders need to make hard decisions
---
Great leaders make great decisions. Period. They can't outsource this tasks. That's what they are hired to do. I myself had been noticing that I was trying to ask for opinions to avoid making decisions myself and blame others. But this doesn't work. You need to come down to a conclusion your own so that, when it comes a time to fix it, you know how to do it.
