title: 🇩🇰 Hygge (hook-ka)
---
#permanent/concept 

A philosophy from the Danish. Hygge means "warmth" as from hugging or well being in general. 

Hygge encourages us to be happy by noticing small happy moments. 
This is similar to [[Ikigai is the reason for being#^a67553|Micro Ikigai]]
