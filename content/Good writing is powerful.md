---
title: Good writing is powerful
---
Powerful writing moves people with stories and actions. You need to have a point of view and express it in a way that has dynamic. 

---
- Use active verbs. Things DO .. not ARE 
- Add people to your writing. 
- Open your writing with strong lead
	- Personal stories
	- Add energy 
- Have an opinion. 
- Be consistent. Make the point in the beginning and keep it throughout your writing
- Set the tone and maintian it. 
- Use dense words with lots of meaning
- Say things in positive ways 
	- **No** He does not show up
	- **Yes** He disappear
