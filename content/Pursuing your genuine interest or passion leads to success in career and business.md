---
title: Pursuing your genuine interest or passion leads to success in career and business
---
#permanent 
- You need to love what you do in order to stick to the "thing" long enough to be successful
- When you love what you do →  you'll be obsessed. 
	- When you're obsessed → you'll get good
	- When you're good → you'll be successful
- Business is hard. Passion will help you be resilient and overcome the obstacles ^43b630
- True curiosity that leads to good career should be life long. If there is a thing that excites you now but you might lose interest in the future, that is not your true curiosity. You need to keep looking.
