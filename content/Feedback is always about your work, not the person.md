---
title: Feedback is always about your work, not the person
---
Feedback is all about improvement. We take and receive feedback in order to get better. So, in order to most effectively take action upon the feedback, we have to see feedback as a response to our work, not our identity. 

You have to start seeing all the comments to the action to what you do that is really detached from you.
