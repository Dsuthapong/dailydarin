---
title: Business is like surfing
---
You need to enjoy the game, not the most glamorus part. 
In surfing, you only spend 5% of your time surfing. Most of the time you will spend in the water, sitting and waiting for the wave to come. You will paddle, miss the wave, and get wiped out from time to time. 

So to enjoy surfing, you need to enjoy your time in the ocean, not just when you surf the wave. Just like in business, you need to enjoy the game, not just when you win.
