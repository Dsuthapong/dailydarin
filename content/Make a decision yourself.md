---
title: Make a decision yourself
---
It’s very important that you should always make a decision yourself.
It’s very good to listen and absorb as much information as possible. However, this doesn’t mean that you should “believe” in everything that people told you, even the experts. 

You should take all the information into consideration and, in the end, decide yourself what to do with that situation. Because, if you follow the recommendation that people gave you blindly, when things go south, you have no idea how to fix the situation. 

**So the key is:**
- Be open and Listen a lot
- Think deeply
- Decide for yourself.. 
[[OODA loop]]
  
  Whether it is a small things as what you should have for lunch, the key strategic decision in your company, all the way to important life-changing decision, it is very important that you make all these decisions yourself. 
  Sure, you should listen to all the information and advice that people gave. You should read widely and be open to new information; However, it is important that in the end, you make the decision yourself. 
  
  Making a decision yourself is scary. Very scary sometimes. But that’s the only way that you can grow as a person and as a company. Without the ability to make a decision yourself, you don’t know where your compass is. What are your values. And these things are super important in growing as a person and as a company.
