---
title: Don’t ignore your intuition when hiring despite great track record
---

When you make a hiring decision, ===Never=== ignore your intuition. If there's anything wrong about the candidate, listen to your gut feeling. You should never let a beautiful resume or convincing track-records fooled you. 

In my past experiences, Ignoring these signs never served me well: 
- **Hiring a dev team:** When I consulted for a big restaurant in Thailand, I had to choose a frontend development team on customer's behalf. I had all the bad signs about this team. The interaction we had with them told me that this team was not professional at all in every single way: the way the communicated, the way they presented, and even how they proposed the price. However, I let all these signs slipped away as they have successfully delivered big projects in the past. And also, the final piece of information that let to eventually hiring this team was a positive comment someone whom worked with this team in the past. The person making this comment was a super credible guy with a high position in a big corporate. 
- **Hiring an EA** I was recommended by an investor that I should hire this person as my executive assistant. This investor is the founder from one of the most respected startup in Thailand (Lazada). I also asked for a reference and got super positive feedback that this person is one of the best EA in the whole World. 

In both examples, the dev team and the EA turned out to be super bad. I spotted something fishy since the interview but I chose to ignore them. 

So, when making a hiring decision, whether an employee or anyone, trust your instinct and dig deeper. No amount of past credibiliy will hide this.
