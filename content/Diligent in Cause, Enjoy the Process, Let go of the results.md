#permanent 
This is the key to have a "healthy success," which is to be happy and also being successful (making progress)
 at the same time. 

Here are the steps: 
1. Set goals & Identify plan 
	- Set where you wanna go, want you want to achieve. 
	- What's the meaning of it? Why you want it? 
	- What you need to do to get it 
2. ==Diligent in cause & Enjoy the process==
	- This is the part you should focus on the most. 
	- Focus on doing the actions and enjoy the process 
	- Don't think about the results. Make the process fun and as enjoyable as possible. 
3. Let go of the results 
4. Reflect at times
	- When the results come out. Use it as "guidance" and "information" to inform the following actions 
	- You can course correct your actions based on the results that come out
		- For example, if the result is good, it means that you should keep going this way
		- If you haven't reached the result, ask your master and seek guidance.
