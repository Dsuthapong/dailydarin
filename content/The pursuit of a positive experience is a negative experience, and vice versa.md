---
title: The pursuit of a positive experience is a negative experience, and vice versa
---
#permanent 
The more you want something, the less like you’ll get it. 

In life, there are relationship between effort and reward: 
1. For mindless tasks, the relationship is linear
2. For complex task, relationship is dimishing return
3. For psychological experience, the relationship is "inverted"
!!

This [[Ability to deal with setback dictates your happiness| helps you deal with set backs]]. Since the person who wants the positive emotional experience and the one who has it is the same, it is important to stay still in order to get the positive experience. 

For example, if you want to be loved, you can’t keep “fighting” or “working” for people to love you. You need to accept that sometimes people will hate you, and that’s okay. That’s how you’ll get loved. Because if you ==want== to be loved, you have already admitted that you are NOT LOVED.
