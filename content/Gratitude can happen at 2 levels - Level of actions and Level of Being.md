---
title: Gratitude can happen at 2 levels - Level of actions and Level of Being
---
#permanent 

- Level of actions: Being happy about one's actions
	- A gratitude towards a bus driver who drove us to the of office
	- A teammate who helped us accomplish tasks
- Level of being: Being happy that one simply exists
	- A mother has gratitude towards a child via the simple existence of the child 
	- Gratitude towards oneself for being alive to experience beautiful things
