title: Set up yourself with a good cognitive environment
---

A good cognitive environment is an environment, whether in a physical or a digital World, that has right amount of kinds of stimuli that helps you achieve your goals. This can also often means an environment that is ==absent== of stimuli. 

For example, software we use really plays an important role of a cognitive environment. It has visual and audio stimuli that we have to process so much daily That's why it is very important to select the right tool for your work.
