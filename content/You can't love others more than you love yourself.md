---
title: "You can't love others more than you love yourself"
---
#permanent 
This is because it's impossible to believe that someone could love us more than we love ourselves. 


You can't love someone at 7/10 if you love yourself at 2/10, because you don't even know what it's like to love at 7/10. You can't go to places you've never been. 

You can "give" to others more than you give to yourself, but that won't come from the place of love, it comes from the place of "wanting something back in return."
