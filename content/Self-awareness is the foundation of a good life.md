title: Self-awareness is the foundation of a good life
---
#permanent 
I used to write "The level of [[Self-awareness]] corresponds to the level of satisfaction in life," but I think now it's more than that. It's the foundation of a good life. 

It helps you 
1. Understand what you want, both at the macro and micro level. Macro level is what you really want in life. Micro level is what you really want at each moment. Also there's something in between like, what you want each week, each state of life... 
2. Know how to navigate life using what's given to you, like your talents.. and avoid blind spots.
