---
title: We should prioritize flow rather than following a rigid structure, if the task is executed with the proper priority
---

- Being in a state that is primed to execute or something (especially important things) is rare. Therefore, when you're in this state, you should consider prioritizing doing this
- ==However, in order to use your time effectively, you first need to know your goals for the week, month, year, in order to make sure that you are on the right track. ==
- *Examples*
	- If this week your primary goal is to finish a pitch deck, and now you are in a time block to clear email, then suddenly you got a clear idea to execute on the deck, in this case you should prioritize doing the deck
	- However, if you are working on a pitch deck in this time block, and then you have an urge to reply an *unimportant* email, you can quickly dump the idea to reply the email into your capturing system  and quickly get back to working on the deck as it is the clear priority of the week.
