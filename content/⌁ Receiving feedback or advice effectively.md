I believe that the skill of accepting feedback/advice well is one of the most important skills today, and years to come. We are living in the age where advice is everywhere, and you can find someone who would agree or disagree with almost anything. Then how can you possibly know what to take, and what to ignore. This Hub note is a place to explore this idea.
