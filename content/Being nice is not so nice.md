What it means to be [[A Nice Guy]]

1. **It limits your potential:** How can you be the best of yourself when you don't even believe you think you worth the good stuff!
2. **You're not too happy!** 
	- You feel resented as you *give* from the place of wanting something back. And when people don't treat you as expected, you blow up! 
	- You live life worrying all the time how others will think of you. 
3. **It prevents you from having a good quality relationship**
	- 	[[A Nice guy is dishonest]]. So you're not authentic and not able to have a deep and real relationship.
