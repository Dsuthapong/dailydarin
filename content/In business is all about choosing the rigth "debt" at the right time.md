title: "In business is all about choosing the rigth \"debt\" at the right time"
---

Along the journey of building a business. You will run into a debt, and it's all about what debt you choose to have and what time. For example, raising fund in startups is all about having financial debts so that you have less of other debts, such as culture debts, technical debts, operational debts. Debts are the things that you have to pay back some day.
