title: Picking the right market is the number-one key to business success
---
No matter what you do, if you are in the "wrong" market, you won't be successful. The market is something that you can't change. A shitty product and team that decently solve problems in a great market will succeed. 

For startups, based on  and also , a good market should: 
- Lots of Pain
- Purchasing Power
- Easy to target (Accessible)
- Growing
