---
title: 6 Levels of delegation
---
#permanent/concept 
!

When people are hesitant to giveaway some of the control, that's because it's scary to giveaway the whole thing to another person. In fact, there could be pieces that can be taken away to delegate

**Level 1: Do What I say**
- This is something basic or you have someone finishing something you have started
**Level2: Look into this for me**
- This is similar to human googling
- You have someone do some research about something for you
**Level3: Give me your advice and I'll decide**
**Level 4: Decide and Check Back with me**
**Level 5: Decide within these limits**
**Level 6: Just get it done**
