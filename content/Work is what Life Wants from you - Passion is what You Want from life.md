title: Work is what Life Wants from you - Passion is what You Want from life
---
- There are not enough jobs to fit "your" passion, so don't worry too much about it. 
- Work is about "surviving" (paying bills)
- Passion is a "self-centered" way of looking at work (what makes me happy)
- You can find passion outside of work
