#permanent 
Community Feeling is the concept from [[Adler Psychology]] It is the sense of belonging to something greater than 

To get Community Feelings we need: 
1. **Self Acceptance:** Accepting oneself as is, so that one can alter the situation in one's ability
2. **Confidence in others:** Seeing others as comrades rather than enemies. Believing in others that they will not take advantage of us. We of course need to employ this carefully
3. **[[Contribution to others is the core to self-worth and happiness|Contribution to others]]** : Make ourselves useful to others
