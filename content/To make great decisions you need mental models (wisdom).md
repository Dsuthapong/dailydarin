title: To make great decisions you need mental models (wisdom)
---
#permanent 
Based on , You need to collect great mental models along your life’s journey to make great decision. When it comes the time of making a decision, especially a tough one, you have a limited time to decide, so you need to collect your wisdom along the way so that when you need it, you can just pull off and use it! 

The mental model is based on past experiences of 1.) What happened 2.) Why it happened. It is impossible to get the model simply from reading quotes on social media. You need an underlying experience to make sense of it. And to get that experience you need to either get it yourself or read others’ experiences.
