title: Great interpersonal relationship is rooted in Confidence instead of Trust
---
#permanent 

Based on [[Adler Psychology]] referred in the book , there are 2 concepts when it comes to interpersonal relationship
1. Trust in others = Has conditions
2. Believing in others/ Confidence in others = No condition
Trust is easily broken, but belief is hard to be broken. 
To have a truly satisfying relationship, you need "Confidence" or "Beliefs"

Mentioned in , Unconditional (Love with no expectation) is what makes us happy, because you are in control (nothing the other person does will make you unhappy).
