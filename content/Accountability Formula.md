Accountability = Expectation+Measurement x Feedback

- Expectation = What does "good" looks like
	- Value = Good at the company level
	- Brand Promise = Good to the customers 
	- Role = Good at the job
- Measurement = How will we measure "good"
- Feedback = Letting your employees know how far they are from "good"
