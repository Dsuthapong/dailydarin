---
title: 7-38-55 Rule
---
It's a rule developed by  professor Albert Mehrabian at the University of California, Los Angeles that only 7% of meaning is communicated through words
- 55% from the body language
- 38% from the vocal tone  
- 7% from the content (Choice of words)
