---
title: "To be productive, you need to optimize for the \"Outcome\" or the \"Output\""
---
#permanent 
You need to define what is the "Product" or the "Outcome" you want and then optimize everything for that outcome. 

Sometimes doing "unproductive" things like sleep or rest, doesn't look "productive" but it is important. [[Don't do anything until you know what to do]]

If you don't define this well, you run into the [[Obvious Target Trap]]
