---
title: People are more convinced when they come to the conclusion themselves instead of being told
---
#permanent 
- People are more likely to believe in things that they come to the conclusion themselves instead of someone telling them what the truth is. 
- Therefore, to convince people, you should present them with compelling facts, BUT let them conclude themselves
- OR Asking questions and letting the person think for themselves

Example: If you want to convince your friend that her BF cheated on her, instead of simply telling the opinion (which she can easily defend) you can ask things like "When was the last time you have a date night?," "What time did he normally come home these days.."
