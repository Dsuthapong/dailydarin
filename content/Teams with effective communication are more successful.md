title: Teams with effective communication are more successful
---
What is good communication  (based on [MIT Study](https://youtu.be/ErKqopYErdk?t=638) of 30 companies. Ones with good communication had higher revenue)
- **Energy** - lots of communications 
- **Engagement** - distribution of communication (among team members)
- **Exploration** - cross teams
