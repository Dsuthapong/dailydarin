#permanent/concept 

[The Ansoff Matrix](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/knowledge/strategy/ansoff-matrix/) is an expansion framework developed by a mathematician in 1957. It breaks down the relationship between a product and the [target market](https://www.newbreedrevenue.com/blog/target-market-vs.-buyer-persona-whats-the-difference) and the riskiness of that combination.

!

-   **Diversification:** Introducing a new product to a new market
-   **Product Development:** Introducing a new product into an existing market
-   **Market Development:** Introducing an existing product into a new market
-   **Market Penetration:** Further selling an existing product into an existing market
