**Ikigai is "the reason for being"**
It's about realizing the **value** of your exisitence, either through the joyful experience in life, or being useful to others.


**2 Types of Ikigai**
- Micro Ikigai: Small happiness in everyday life, such as eating good food, meeting great people, seeing the silver lining in an otherwise bad situation ^a67553
	- [[⌁ Gratitude]] 
- Macro Ikigai: The significant feeling of being valuable and useful to others, such as a meaningful work 
	- To find Major Ikigai, you need to master Micro Ikigai. It's like you a are a magnet and you have power to align mass to your polar [[Law of attraction]]
