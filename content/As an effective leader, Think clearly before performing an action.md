---
title: As an effective leader, Think clearly before performing an action
---
Leaders need to communicate and motivate people. There will be a lot of downstream impact, so thinking critically is very important. 

Concentrate on the work in front of you
Manage emotion. You’re already worthwhile. You don’t need to be liked. 
Give a some time to think.
Ideate in your thinking.
