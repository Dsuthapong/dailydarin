title: Horizontal relationship is the key to happy interpersonal interaction
---
#permanent 
There are 2 kinds of relationships: Horizontal and Vertical. Vertical means one person is above another person. Horizontal relationship means that everyone is equal in value. 

When we develop a Horizontal relationship, once person doesn't  need the approval from another person. Each person is free.
