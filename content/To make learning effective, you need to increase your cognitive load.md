# To make learning effective, you need to increase your cognitive load
This could be done by trying to hold on information in your head for a longer period of time before you offload by note-taking [[⌁ How writing will make you be more successful]].

This is contrasted with [[Empty your mind to be in a beautiful state]]. If things in your head are not very useful, then you should empty them.
