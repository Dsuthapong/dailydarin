[[WIIFM - What's In It For Me]]
- Interest
- Need: 
	- Main needs: The key content they want
	- Current needs: The immediately minor needs, such as feeling bored, needed a break or go to rest room
- Influence: Their beliefs or anything that already influenced them (Related to [[To influence people, you need to know what already influence them|Tony Robbin's saying]])
	- Positive Influence: Who or what they like
	- Negative influence: Who or what they hate
