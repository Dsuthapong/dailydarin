#permanent 

Failure is helpful. It helps you learn. It builds strengths. But [[Success]] is better. Don't romanticize failure. It's okay that it happens, but you also don't "aim" for it.


[[Fear setting]] is one of the concepts that show that failure and help prepare success.

 is also a really great way to let the "failure" sets the tone of the success.
