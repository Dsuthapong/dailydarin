#permanent 

Here are some ways: 
1. Tell a shocking fact that is relevant to the audience
2. Set a Question that matters to the audience
3. Tell a personal story.
	1. Story has people in it. People love hearing about other people
	2. Tell different kinds of stories depending on what kind of feelings you want to trigger 
4. Quotes 
5. Jokes
