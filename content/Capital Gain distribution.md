#permanent/concept 
Capital gains distribution refers to the payment that a mutual fund or exchange-traded fund (ETF) makes to its shareholders from the profits it has earned by selling securities in its portfolio at a higher price than what it paid for them. These distributions are typically made annually and are subject to taxes. They reflect the fund's realized gains, which are the profits from sold securities, rather than the overall increase in the value of the fund's holdings.
