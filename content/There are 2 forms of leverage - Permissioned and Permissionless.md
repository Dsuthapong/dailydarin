#permanent 

Permissioned Leverage needs to wait for others to give permission, such as money or labor
Permissionless Leverage is the kind of leverage that you can use without waiting for others to give you permission

In the , Naval said: 
Permissioned Leverage = Money and Labor 
Permissionless Leverage = Code and Media

But actually, another kind of Permissionless Leverage is being very good at something.
