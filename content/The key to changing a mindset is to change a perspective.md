# The key to changing a mindset is to change a perspective

- Perspective is how you see things. For example, do you see you life as actions in a to-do list or a 30-year journey you have left. 
- To start changing someone’s course of actions, you need to change the perspective by influencing someone. [[Leadership is the skill of influencing people, including yourself]]
