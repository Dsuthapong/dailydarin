# To get good and be dangerous at something you only takes 20 hours
Making popular by a book , the concept of being an expert takes 10,000 hours is widely spread everywhere. 
But the truth from the TED Talk  is that it only takes 20 hours to get to the point that you can be pretty good at something. 

However, you need to take [[Deliberate practice]] to make it effective: 
1. You need to deconstruct an important skills to learn into very clear components. 
2.  Get good enough to self-correct 
3. Get rid of distraction
4. Spend 20 hours
