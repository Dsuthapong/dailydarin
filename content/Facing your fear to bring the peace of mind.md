When you are afraid of something, it means that you are afraid of “pain.” Often times it is “emotional pain” rather than physical pain.

In, he mentioned that we should set “Fear” instead of setting goals. Setting fears gives you: 
1. Ability to think through potential challenges
2. Mindset to prepare for unexpected events
3. Peace of mind, knowing that you have think through and done every thing you can to prevent them 

[In this podcast by Alex Lieberman, the co-founder of Morning Brew](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6YpJ2HhBnpmPdZIefO3lPX?si=dZxOfIgWT2Owoj0ojWf6ew),  he also mentioned the concept of “Inversion” where instead of setting goals with “Top-Down” or “Bottom-up” thinking, you should think about “What have to happen in order for you to FAIL.” This allows you to be much more prepared and more thoughtful about the things you do.
