---
title: You need the courage to be happy and be okay to be disliked
---
#permanent 
You don't lack the ==ability== to be happy. You just lack the courage.

The courage to be happy entails that you need to live lives on your own terms, embrace freedom, and escape the conformity. This comes at the cost of ==being disliked sometimes==!

If one doesn't have the courage to be disliked, one will have to conform to the society to get recognition. One can't live freely. 

One needs to practice [[Separation of Tasks]]. Wanting to be liked is your task, but liking or disliking is others' tasks. If one can understand this and don't intervene the tasks, the relationship moves to lightness.
