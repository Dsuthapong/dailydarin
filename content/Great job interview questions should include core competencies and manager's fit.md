There are 5 core competencies:
1. *Motivation:* What drives them [[6 basic Human needs are- Certainty, Variety, Significance, Connection, Growth, Giving]]
2. *Initiation*: Do they love "starting" or "maintaining"
3. Work Style: Alone, Team? 
4. Relationship: What kind of people and how much people involvement
5. Thought Process: How they think
