---
title: You should niche down your product to solve specific problems so that you can charge more
---
#permanent 
The more specific your stuff is, the more you can charge, but lesser units you can charge as well.

Example: 
- Time management course: 19 USD
- Time management for sales people: 100 USD 

 also talks about this concept that you should NOT niche your audience, but niche your product.
