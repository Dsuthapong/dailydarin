---
title: To take notes effectively, you need to understand the meaning and jot down key words
---
You need to listen to figure out the meaning and jot down just the parts to remind your understanding:
1. Don't take word-by-word
2. Don't even take "some words" verbatim, as it doesn't mean anything
3. Add flesh within 24 hours
4. Don't type (as you'll get more distracted)
