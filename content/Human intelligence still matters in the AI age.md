---
title: Human intelligence still matters in the AI age
---
 While now we can use chat-gpt to do virtually many things, we still need to cultivate the skill of being "sharp," thinking well on the spot, [[⌁ How to write well|writing well]], so that we can "connect" and "influence" people effectively. Imagine you have to waiting ask Chat GPT every time you have to send email or make important conversation, I think that's not really realistic, nor desirable. 

We should use AI to advance and cultivate our thinking, not constantly use it to edit our thoughts.
