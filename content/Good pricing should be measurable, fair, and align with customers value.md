---
title: Good pricing should be measurable, fair, and align with customers value
---
#permanent 

1. **Measurable:** Can objectively be measured. For example, a "satisfied customer" is hard to be measured and shouldn't be used for as the pricing metric
2. **Fair:** Customers need to expect to pay it 
3. **Align with customers value:** The more they pay should be the more value they get
