title: The Sandwich effect (energy)
---
#permanent/concept 

Mentioned , The Sanwich effect is about hiding the things you don't like to do in the middle of 2 things you like to do.
For example, if you don't like doing tax, you can put it in the middle of 2 activities you like: 
- Listen to your fav song
- Do tax
- Eat ice-cream
