title: Commodity VS Differentiated Pricing
---
#permanent 
There are mainly 2 ways to charge, commodity and differentiated pricing. 

**Commodity pricing:** Pricing based on competitors' offers
==**Differentiated pricing:** Pricing is so different, that is hard to compare.==

- Differentiated pricing allows to to have more profit as it sort of creates a  for your offer. You don't have to compete. 
- It sends the signal to the customers that this is not the same as the competitors
